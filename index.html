About me

I'm a second year maths PhD student in the number theory group at the University of Sheffield. My supervisor is Tobias Berger. Before that, I did my undergraduate degree at the University of Bristol.

My PhD project is looking at how torsion in the cohomology of arithmetic groups is related to certain Galois deformations, more specifically it aims to prove a 'weak $R=\mathbb{T}$ theorem'. I work mostly in the setting of imaginary quadratic fields, where algebro-geometric methods tend to break down.

I'm interested in the algebraic and representation-theoretic aspects of number theory, including Galois deformations, Selmer groups, GMA representations, anabelian goemetry, and K-theory.

My master's project was supervised by Ross Paterson, and was on local fields and local class field theory, more specifically the approach via Tate-modified Galois cohomology.

Outside of maths, I enjoy playing guitar, going for walks, and learning languages (currently Gaeilge, ach níl ach cúpla focail agam).
