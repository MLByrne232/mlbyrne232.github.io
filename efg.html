The étale fundamental groups learning seminar is taking place in the autumn semester of the 2026/27 academic year at the University of Sheffield, beginning 8th October 2026. This will mainly be following Tamás Szamuely's excellent book Galois Groups and Fundamental Groups, but we're not doing anything extremely specialised so any reference should work.

We'll be meeting on Thursdays at 13:00 in Hicks J11. The rough talk timeline (subject to change!) is:

08/10/2026 Motivation and examples Mark Chambers
15/10/2026 Schemes Diego Hernandez-Kent
22/10/2026 Special morphisms of schemes Diego Hernandez-Kent
05/11/2026 Geometric points and the definition of $\pi_1^{\textrm{ét}}$ Brais Gerpe Vilas
12/11/2026 $\pi_1^{\textrm{ét}}$ as a profinite group Matt Byrne
19/11/2026 Some particularly interesting examples Matt Byrne
03/12/2026 Galois theory for finite étale covers Hao Zhang
10/12/2026 Outer Galois actions Mark Chambers

Notes will be updated a little bit after the talk, and they'll be shared via the GitHub page. There is an abstract list for those interested, where I've included some possible extra talks we might do after the Christmas break.
